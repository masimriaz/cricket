🏏 About the Event

Freedom Cup 2026 is a one-day community celebration of cricket on America's Independence Day. What began as a small community idea has grown into something truly exciting — 10 teams, 20 matches, 3 pitches running simultaneously, and hundreds of players, families, and spectators coming together for a day of sport, culture, and togetherness.

This event is held at the Eagles' ground — a venue that has been home to so much cricket passion — and we felt it only right to invite someone who is part of this cricketing family to be at the heart of it.
