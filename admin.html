Pitch Manager Duties — Every Match Checklist
  1. 10 min before: Call both teams to pitch
  2. 5 min before: Conduct toss, confirm 9 players each
  3. Start: Signal umpires to begin, start timer
  4. Innings break: 5 minutes max, keep teams ready
  1. Monitor: 27-min over-rate limit per innings
  2. End: Collect scorecard from scorer
  3. Report: Send result to Asim/Ali immediately
  4. Next: Announce next match teams within 5 min
⚠️ KEY RULE: Max 15 min changeover between matches. If a team is late, start 10-min countdown — forfeit if not ready. No exceptions. We must finish on time!
