Match Day Execution (3 Parallel Pitches)

Ali coordinates all 3 pitches — schedule, scoring, disputes. Yousaf, Raza, Munir (seniors) handle team communication and ensure teams reach their pitches on time.

🏏 Ali — Match Coordinator
  • ✓ Coordinate match schedule across all 3 pitches
  • ✓ Ensure matches start & end on time
  • ✓ Scoring oversight & result collection
  • ✓ Dispute resolution (first point of contact)
  • ✓ Enforce over-rate (27 min limit)
  • ✓ Report results to Asim/Hammad
🤝 Seniors — Team Communication

Yousaf, Raza, Munir

  • ✓ Communicate with team captains
  • ✓ Ensure teams ready 5 min before match
  • ✓ Coordinate toss with both teams
  • ✓ Relay schedule changes to teams
  • ✓ Escalation point if Ali needs support
